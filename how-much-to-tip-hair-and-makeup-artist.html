How Much to Tip Your Hair and Makeup Artist

Last reviewed October 2026 · Etiquette

The short answer

A commonly cited range for wedding beauty services is 15% to 20% of the service total, similar to other salon services. Treat it as a guideline, not a rule. Good service, a long day or a difficult schedule can all justify tipping toward the higher end.

Check the contract first

Some artists include gratuity or a service charge in the price. Read your contract so you do not tip twice by accident.

Who to tip

  • The lead artist. Tip the person in charge, who often shares it with assistants.
  • Assistants. If you want to tip them directly, ask the lead artist how they prefer it handled.
  • Business owners. Some people skip tipping owners, but many owners still appreciate it. Follow your own comfort and budget.

When and how to pay

Hand the tip over at the end of the service. Put cash in labelled envelopes ahead of time, or ask if the artist accepts digital payment. Ask a bridesmaid or your planner to handle it if you will be busy.

Add tips to your wedding budget early so they are not a last-minute surprise.
