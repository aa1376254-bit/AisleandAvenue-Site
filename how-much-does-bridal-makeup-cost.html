How Much Does Bridal Makeup Cost in 2026?

Last reviewed October 2026 · Costs

There is no single price

Bridal makeup pricing depends on who is doing it, where you are and how much work the day involves. Quotes for the same look can differ widely between two artists in the same city. The useful question is not "what is the average?" but "what is included in this quote?"

What changes the price

  • Artist experience and demand. Well-booked artists with strong portfolios usually charge more, especially for peak wedding dates.
  • Location. Large metro areas are generally more expensive than smaller cities.
  • Travel. Many artists add a fee to come to your venue or hotel.
  • Early start times. Sunrise appointments often carry a surcharge.
  • Group size. Mothers, bridesmaids and flower girls are usually priced separately.
  • Extras. Lashes, airbrush finish and touch-up kits may be add-ons.

What a good quote includes

  1. Services for each person, listed clearly
  2. Trial cost, and whether it is credited toward your booking
  3. Travel and early-start fees
  4. Deposit amount and the cancellation policy
  5. Whether touch-ups during the day are included

Ways to save without cutting corners

  • Book the trial and the wedding day with the same artist.
  • Ask about off-peak dates or weekday weddings.
  • Get the bride's service from your first-choice artist and use a simpler package for guests.
  • Compare quotes from three artists using the same checklist above.

Always get the final price in writing before paying a deposit.
